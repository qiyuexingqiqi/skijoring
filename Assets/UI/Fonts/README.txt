AlpineUI.ttf is a UI-only subset of Noto Sans SC (weight 500).
Source: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/notosanssc
License: SIL Open Font License 1.1 (see OFL.txt).
ASCII and the Chinese characters used in EquipmentSelectionBuilder, SkiRaceSceneBuilder, SkiRaceManager, SkiWeatherSceneBuilder and SkiGameplayHUD are bundled so equipment selection, weather, gameplay, race timer and completion UI do not depend on installed system fonts.
